Index weights

The Food Security Index (FSI, 0–100) is a weighted mean of five pillar scores. Weights are normalized automatically; set a pillar to 0 to drop it.

🟫 Land
💧 Water

Pillar definitions

Production — cereal production per capita, log-scaled 50 → 1,500 kg/cap/yr.

Land — arable land per capita, log-scaled 0.01 → 1.5 ha/cap.

Water — ½ total renewable water per capita (log 100 → 20,000 m³/cap/yr) + ½ (1 − SDG 6.4.2 water stress, capped at 100%).

Trade — 0.5 × cereal self-reliance (import dependency +100% → −100% mapped 0 → 100) + 0.3 × food-import burden (food imports as % of merchandise exports, log 2% → 100% mapped 100 → 0) + 0.2 × net virtual-water position (+1,500 → −1,500 m³/cap mapped 0 → 100).

Access — GDP per capita, PPP (constant 2021 int. $), log-scaled 1,000 → 60,000.

Fixed bounds (not dataset min–max) keep scores comparable when data change. A country gets an FSI only when pillars covering ≥ 60% of the total weight have data; available pillars are re-weighted.

Sources, vintages & coverage

Harmonization notes

  • Keys. All sources joined on UN M49 / ISO 3166-1 numeric codes (World Bank ISO3 converted). Water Footprint Network country names were mapped to ISO codes by hand where needed.
  • Territorial changes. Water footprint values for the former Sudan (pre-2011) are shown for both Sudan and South Sudan, and for former Serbia and Montenegro for both Serbia and Montenegro; virtual-water totals for those cannot be split and are left blank. Channel Islands data are drawn on Jersey and Guernsey; Somaliland is drawn with Somalia; Kosovo is matched by name.
  • China. FAOSTAT "China, mainland" is used for China; Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macao appear separately where sources report them.
  • Sector shares are each sector's withdrawal divided by the sum of the three sectors (AQUASTAT totals can include minor unallocated volumes). Industry includes thermoelectric cooling.
  • AQUASTAT flags. Most AQUASTAT values in the 2023 dissemination are carried forward or modelled (flag E = estimate, I = imputed, A = official). The flag is shown in each country profile.
  • Missing or zero values. AQUASTAT reports a renewable-resources value of 0 for some micro-states it has not assessed; these are treated as missing. FAOSTAT values shown as "<2.5%" are stored as 2.5. Cereal production last reported before 2015 is treated as missing.
  • Irrigation share = AQUASTAT area equipped for full-control irrigation ÷ World Bank arable land (capped at 100%, since irrigated permanent crops can push it above arable area).
  • Water footprint vintage. Footprint and virtual-water figures are the most recent complete global national accounts (1996–2005 averages, Mekonnen & Hoekstra 2011). Treat them as structural indicators, not current values.
